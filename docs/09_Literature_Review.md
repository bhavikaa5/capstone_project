# Literature Review — 9 Papers

Scoped to the current project objectives: HMM-based regime labeling, CNN-BiLSTM-Attention / LSTM / Transformer comparison for regime classification, hyperparameter tuning, and live validation on Indian (Nifty 50) intraday data.

| # | Paper (Link) | Title | Key Points | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [SSRN 3406068](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3406068) | Market Regime Identification Using Hidden Markov Models — Yuan & Mitra (2016) | Applies a Gaussian HMM to FTSE 100 and Euro Stoxx 50 return series; captures fat tails and volatility clustering better than Geometric Brownian Motion; identifies distinct regimes for factor-switching strategies. | Regime detection only — no coupling to a downstream deep-learning classifier or trading system; not applied to Indian equities; no live validation. |
| 2 | [Neural Computing and Applications, Springer (2020)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00521-020-05532-z) | A CNN-BiLSTM-AM Method for Stock Price Prediction — Lu, Li, Li, Sun & Wang | Combines CNN feature extraction, BiLSTM for bidirectional temporal dependencies, and an attention mechanism to weight important time steps; beats plain LSTM/MLP/SVM baselines. | Formulated as price-level regression, not regime classification; no comparison against a Transformer; no HMM-based ground-truth labels; not tested on Indian markets. |
| 3 | [arXiv:2603.19136](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19136) | Adaptive Regime-Aware Stock Price Prediction Using Autoencoder-Gated Dual Node Transformers with Reinforcement Learning Control — Al Ridhawi, Haj Ali & Al Osman (2026) | Autoencoder flags anomalous/regime-shifted states via reconstruction error; dual-node Transformers specialize for stable vs. event-driven regimes; an RL controller tunes the regime threshold and pathway blending; tested on 20 S&P 500 stocks (1982–2025). | Very heavy architecture (autoencoder + dual Transformer + RL controller) — not reproducible in one semester; still price-prediction, not a labeled classification task; no dedicated HMM ground truth; U.S.-only, no live intraday validation. |
| 4 | [arXiv:2411.05790](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05790) | Comparative Analysis of LSTM, GRU, and Transformer Models for Stock Price Prediction — Xiao, Bi & Deng (2024) | Direct head-to-head comparison of LSTM, GRU, and Transformer architectures on Tesla stock data (2015–2024). | Single stock, U.S. market; regression task, not classification; no regime labels or HMM grounding; no hyperparameter tuning study; no live validation. |
| 5 | [ScienceDirect (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666827025001136) | Integrating Deep Learning and Econometrics for Stock Price Prediction: A Comprehensive Comparison of LSTM, Transformers, and Traditional Time Series Models | Benchmarks LSTM and Transformer models against classical econometric models (e.g., ARIMA) for stock price forecasting. | Compares against classical baselines, not against CNN-BiLSTM-Attention; not a regime-classification task; no Indian-market or live-data validation. |
| 6 | [Research Square, preprint (2025)](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-7567095/v1) | Impact of Hyperparameter Optimisation Techniques in Deep Learning-based Investment Predictions: An Indian ETF-based Analysis | Compares grid search, Bayesian optimization, Optuna GridSampler, and Optuna TPE for tuning LSTM and 1D-CNN on the Nifty BeEs ETF (2010–2025); evaluates with RMSE, directional accuracy, and Sharpe ratio; Optuna-TPE gives the best results. | Only tunes LSTM and 1D-CNN — no CNN-BiLSTM-Attention or Transformer; tunes for price prediction, not regime classification; no HMM-based regime labels; not validated live. |
| 7 | [IEEE Xplore (2022)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9754148/) | Stock Market Analysis and Prediction for Nifty50 using LSTM Deep Learning Approach | Applies LSTM directly to Nifty 50 index historical data for price-trend prediction. | Single architecture (LSTM only) on daily data; no regime detection/HMM component; no comparison with CNN-BiLSTM-Attention or Transformer; no hyperparameter tuning; no live intraday (15-min) validation. |
| 8 | [MDPI (2024)](https://www.mdpi.com/2674-1032/3/4/29) | Comparative Analysis of Deep Learning Models for Stock Price Prediction in the Indian Market | Compares RNN, LSTM, CNN, GRU, and Attention-LSTM on Indian equities/Nifty 50 using MAE, MSE, R². | Regression metrics only (price-level accuracy), not regime/state classification; excludes Transformer and CNN-BiLSTM-Attention combined architecture; no trading-performance validation; no live data. |
| 9 | [Evolving Systems, Springer (2022)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12530-022-09481-x) | An Efficient Real-Time Stock Prediction Exploiting Incremental Learning and Deep Learning | Proposes an incremental/online learning framework that continuously updates a deep-learning model with live streaming data for real-time stock prediction. | Focused on incremental retraining strategy, not regime classification; not evaluated jointly on classification + trading-performance metrics; not tested on Nifty 50 or 15-minute Indian market data. |

## How these map to the project objectives

| Objective | Supporting papers | What the gap tells us to build |
|---|---|---|
| 1. HMM regime labeling + DL training on historical data | 1, 7, 8 | Paper 1 proves HMM is a strong regime-labeling tool but stops at detection; papers 7–8 show deep models work on Nifty 50 but only for price prediction, never regime labels. **We combine both** — HMM labels feeding supervised classifiers. |
| 2. Compare CNN-BiLSTM-Attention, LSTM, Transformer | 2, 3, 4, 5 | Each paper studies at most two of these architectures, on price prediction, never as a three-way regime-classification comparison. **We run all three, head-to-head, on the same regime-labeled data.** |
| 3. Hyperparameter tuning for accuracy + trading performance | 6 | Paper 6 shows Optuna-TPE tuning works on Indian ETF data but only for LSTM/1D-CNN price prediction. **We extend tuning to all three architectures, optimizing for regime accuracy and trading performance jointly.** |
| 4. Live validation on 15-min Nifty 50 data | 9 | Paper 9 shows incremental live prediction is feasible but never reports classification + trading metrics together. **We validate the winning model live, on both fronts at once.** |

## Note on source integrity

One additional Optuna/LSTM hyperparameter-tuning paper (Ismanto & Vitriani, *JITEKI*, 2023) was found during search but **excluded — it has been formally retracted by the publisher for misconduct and plagiarism**. Do not cite it. Paper 6 above is a clean, non-retracted replacement covering the same technique (Optuna hyperparameter optimization) on a comparable Indian-market dataset.
